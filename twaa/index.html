The Route
Customer App · Abu El Matamir

Interactive design prototype of the customer app (BRD v1.1), scoped to Abu El Matamir and its villages. Arabic-first, RTL, noon Minutes-style pattern: big ETA header, search, hero offers, dense category grid, quick-add carousels, sticky cart bar, cart in the tab bar. Location screen uses your real GPS and a draggable map with live zone detection.
